# Base game tasks (15)

Each task opens the scenario's own park with its own goal (checked on Prime: the game reports these goal types and numbers). Same prompt for all ([PROMPT.md](PROMPT.md)); only the name and goal change. Pass = the game itself says the scenario is completed (OpenRCT2 v0.5.5 ScenarioObjective.cpp). Time cap: 60 real minutes for now.

| # | Scenario | Difficulty | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Electric Fields | Beginner | Have **at least 700 guests in the park at the end of October, Year 2**. The park rating must also be at least 600 on that date. |
| 2 | Factory Capers | Beginner | Have **at least 1,100 guests in the park at the end of October, Year 3**. The park rating must also be at least 600 on that date. |
| 3 | Crazy Castle | Beginner | Have **at least 1,500 guests in the park at the end of October, Year 4**. The park rating must also be at least 600 on that date. |
| 4 | Dusty Greens | Beginner | Earn **£5,000 or more from ride tickets in one game month**. Shop, food and entry money do not count. No deadline. |
| 5 | Bumbly Bazaar | Beginner | Earn **£1,000 or more from food and drink sales in one game month**. No deadline. |
| 6 | Infernal Views | Challenging | Have **10 different types of roller coaster open, each with an excitement rating of at least 6.00**. |
| 7 | Lucky Lake | Challenging | Have **at least 2,100 guests in the park** with a **park rating of at least 700**. No deadline. If the rating stays under 700 for 4 weeks in a row (from the second month on), the game ends it as failed. |
| 8 | Botany Breakers | Challenging | Earn **£10,000 or more from ride tickets in one game month**. Shop, food and entry money do not count. No deadline. |
| 9 | Alpine Adventures | Challenging | Earn **£7,000 or more from ride tickets in one game month**. Shop, food and entry money do not count. No deadline. |
| 10 | Gravity Gardens | Expert | **Pay off the bank loan** and reach a **park value of at least £500,000**. |
| 11 | Extreme Heights | Expert | Have **at least 4,500 guests in the park** with a **park rating of at least 700**. No deadline. If the rating stays under 700 for 4 weeks in a row (from the second month on), the game ends it as failed. |
| 12 | Amity Airfield | Expert | Have **at least 3,000 guests in the park at the end of October, Year 4**. The park rating must also be at least 600 on that date. |
| 13 | Ghost Town | Expert | Have **10 different types of roller coaster open, each with an excitement rating of at least 7.00 and a track length of at least 1,200 m**. |
| 14 | Fungus Woods | Expert | Have **at least 2,500 guests in the park at the end of October, Year 4**. The park rating must also be at least 600 on that date. |
